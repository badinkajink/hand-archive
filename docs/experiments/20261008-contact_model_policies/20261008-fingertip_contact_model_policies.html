Terms and metrics

arm
One fingertip contact model the policies train on. All arms share the hand (real_v1 D6), the working plant (finger servo kp 4 N m/rad, damping 0.08 N m s/rad, torque limit 1 N m), the tool (the 24.5 g screwdriver, = 1), the solver (MuJoCo-Warp, 2 ms, 10 Newton iterations, elliptic cones, impratio 10), the recipe and its flags, and differ only in the fingertip.
env step, iteration
One env step is one 20 ms policy step of one of the 2,048 parallel envs; one PPO iteration collects 24 steps per env, 49,152 env steps. 40 M env steps are 813 iterations.
held (load test)
At a policy step, at least two fingertips each press on the tool with at least 0.24 N (its weight) and the tool is above 60 mm. A rollout is held when this is true at its last step (5 s).
cos
The signed cosine of the tool’s own axis with world vertical, ; +1 is tip down, the goal of the turn; the tool starts horizontal (cos 0).
held cosine
The mean of the final cos over the held rollouts of one evaluation. The turn the policy achieved, counted only where the hand still carries the tool.
evaluation
64 rollouts of the deterministic policy (its mean action) in parallel envs for 250 policy steps, with the training’s timing (residual and reorientation reward from step 58, after the scripted grasp and lift), no early termination and no randomisation. The 64 differ only through the GPU contact solve.
seed spread
Range of the three seeds’ held cosines (or held fractions) of one arm.
rollout spread
Standard deviation of the final cos over the held rollouts of one evaluation, averaged over seeds.
grip
Sum of the three fingertips’ net contact force on the tool, mean over the held steps from step 58, N.
|Δa|
Mean absolute change of the 9-dimensional action between consecutive policy steps, in action units (one unit is 0.5 rad of servo target); 0 for a policy that holds still.
contacts per step
Fingertip-tool contacts summed over the three tips (the fingertip sensor’s match count), mean over the held steps.
penetration
Deepest fingertip-tool penetration over a rollout’s held steps, mm; mean over held rollouts.
creep
Displacement of the tool against the palm over the last second of the rollout divided by 0.98 s (mm/s), and the rotation of its axis over the same second (°/s); median over held rollouts.
peak force
Largest single-fingertip net force over a rollout’s held steps, N; mean over held rollouts.
transfer gap
Held fraction (or held cosine) under another contact model or simulator minus that under the policy’s own contact model in MuJoCo-Warp.
open-loop replay
From the state at policy step 58 of a MuJoCo-Warp rollout, the policy’s recorded finger targets are played into another simulator at 50 Hz with the palm welded at its lifted pose (scripts/rl_policy_replay.py); the policy does not see that simulator’s state.

Contact models and training cost

The legacy box tip is the fingertip every policy of this program trained on through 2026-10-05: one box geom of 10.55×21.1×15 mm per tip, friction 1 (torsional and rolling coefficients present but inactive at condim 3), MuJoCo point contact. The TPU arms replace it by the printed tip, a 17×14.8×22 mm block with 2.7 mm fillets. Condim 4 bounds the spin torque of a point contact by with , the pads’ twist-onset arm on the TPU tip at the policies’ grip: 2.52, 2.74, 2.96 and 3.12 mm at 1, 3, 10 and 20 N (twist_onset/tip_T2.jsonl), 3.0 mm at the typical 12 N. The skin is the presliding candidate kept on the native-compliance page (docs/experiments/20261007-native_compliance/20261007-native_presliding_compliance.html): each tip’s pads sit on a child body joined by two slides tangent to the palmar face and a hinge about its normal, with Mindlin’s and Lubkin’s initial stiffness , on the pads’ own patch at 12 N ( = 3.66 mm, 65 kN/m and 0.90 N m/rad) and critical damping. Three changes from the bed version were needed on the hand: impratio 1000 diverges under the trainer’s ten solver iterations for the pads and the skin alike, so the skin keeps the env’s 10; MuJoCo-Warp threw the tool within 0.1 s at the bed’s armature (spring period 3.6), so the armature is set to = 1 (0.26 kg on the slides, acting on the skin’s own micrometre motion only); and its broadphase needs 768 contact slots per world.
